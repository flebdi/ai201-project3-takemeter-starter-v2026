**Key, with the rule that decides each**

1. `hot_take`: a prediction with nothing behind it.
2. `analysis`: the specific number supports the point being made.
3. `reaction`: a genuine question; the poster doesn't know the answer.
4. `hot_take`: a rhetorical question that implies its own answer.
5. `hot_take`: sarcasm that takes a position. The quote is the target, not
   the evidence.
6. `analysis`: same target, but now a checkable fact backs the jab.
7. `reaction`: a story told for its own sake; no wider point is argued.
8. `analysis`: a measured first-hand result used to support a general claim.

The pairs 1/2, 3/4, 5/6 and 7/8 differ in exactly one thing. Those
differences are the decision rules in the README.
