analysis: makes a point and backs it with something specific that supports that point - a number, a quote used as evidence, a named fact or source, a measured first-hand result, or a step-by-step explanation of how something works
hot_take: takes a position - a judgment, prediction, rhetorical question or sarcastic jab - without offering specific support for it
reaction: takes no arguable position - a feeling, a joke, a genuine question, a story told for its own sake, or a pointer to something else
